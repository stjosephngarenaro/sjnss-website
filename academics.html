Our Learning Facilities

School Library

Our library provides a quiet and conducive environment for independent study. Features include:

  • Over 5,000 reference books and textbooks.
  • Daily newspapers and educational periodicals.
  • Spacious seating for over 60 students at a time.

Modern Laboratories

We believe in practical learning. Our specialized labs are equipped for NECTA practical exams:

  • Physics & Chemistry: Fully stocked with apparatus and chemicals.
  • Biology: Microscopes and biological specimens for study.
  • ICT Lab: Modern computers for digital literacy training.
