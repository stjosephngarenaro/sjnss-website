Welcome to St. Joseph Ngarenaro

Located in the serene landscape of Kisongo, St. Joseph Ngarenaro Girls Secondary School provides a peaceful and dedicated environment for academic excellence. Just a short drive from Arusha city center, our school serves as a gateway to both knowledge and character development.

We focus on nurturing young women to become the leaders of tomorrow through a rigorous curriculum, spiritual growth, and a supportive community atmosphere.

Safe Environment

A secure, tranquil campus in Kisongo designed for focused learning.

Academic Rigor

High standards across all subjects with a focus on science and arts.

Girl-Centered

Dedicated to the unique empowerment and education of young women.
